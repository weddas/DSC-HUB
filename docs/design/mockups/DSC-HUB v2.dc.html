3Scaling model — one plant → one tent → room of tents → rooms; tents that flip roles

The model: everything is a Zone (tent, container, rack, room). Zones nest: Site › Room (lung) › Zone › Plant. A zone has a role that can flip without re-creating it: Grow (Veg / Gen / Bulk / Finish) → Dry → Cure → Empty. The role decides which sensors matter, which bands apply, which appliances are allowed to act, and which log lines are written. Density is decided by the count at each level, never by the user picking a "mode": 1 zone shows plants; 2–6 show tent cards with the triad; 7–30 collapse to compact cards grouped by their lung; 30+ become a strip table with a heat column. Controls scale the same way: one tent gets direct toggles and a shot editor; many zones get recipes and rules applied to a selection, with per-zone override shown as a diff.
