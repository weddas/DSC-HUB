Medical Cannabis Dispensaries in Australia

Australia's medical cannabis program allows patients with valid prescriptions to access cannabis products through authorised dispensaries and pharmacies. Our directory helps you find dispensaries that specialise in medical cannabis products including oils, flowers, capsules, and other therapeutic formulations.

What to Look for in a Cannabis Dispensary

  • Specialised Knowledge: Staff trained in medical cannabis products and their applications
  • Product Availability: Consistent stock of prescribed medications
  • RRP (Recommended Retail Price): Pharmacies that honour recommended retail pricing
  • Professional Service: Proper handling and dispensing of medical cannabis products

States and Territories Covered

Our directory covers dispensaries across all Australian states and territories including New South Wales (NSW), Queensland (QLD), Victoria (VIC), Western Australia (WA), South Australia (SA), Tasmania (TAS), Australian Capital Territory (ACT), and Northern Territory (NT).
