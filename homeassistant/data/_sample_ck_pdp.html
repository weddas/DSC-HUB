What is Night Moves Strain?

Night Moves Strain is a premium Indica-Dominant Hybrid crafted from two exceptional parent strains, Midnight Runtz and Scotty 2 Hotty. With a notable 20% THC content, it offers potency without overwhelming strength, making it a versatile choice for collectors and enthusiasts alike. The strain’s low CBD level of 0.28% keeps the focus on the robust qualities provided by its Indica-dominant profile, bringing a sense of calm and relaxation. Combining the deep, flavorful nature of Midnight Runtz with the unique genetics of Scotty 2 Hotty, Night Moves Strain boasts a complex flavor profile that combines subtle sweetness with earthy undertones.

This strain’s genetics make it a true standout, balancing well-rounded effects with a pleasant aroma and taste. Night Moves Strain is ideal for those looking to unwind at the end of the day, while its manageable THC level appeals to both experienced and newer collectors. This strain captures the best characteristics of its parent strains, resulting in a balanced experience with relaxing effects.

Growing Information of Night Moves Strain

Night Moves Strain is appreciated by growers for its compact structure and steady yields, fitting well into indoor and outdoor environments. This strain thrives best in temperate climates, keeping within a moderate 0-4 ft height range, which is ideal for more discreet or space-limited grows. Indoor cultivators can expect yields of up to 600 grams per square meter, making it a productive strain for smaller spaces. Outdoors, each plant provides around 350-400 grams, with harvest time falling in October.

With a flowering time of 8-10 weeks, Night Moves Strain fits well within a reasonable grow cycle, delivering a quality yield without an extended waiting period. Its compact height makes it easy to manage in indoor setups, while its robust nature makes it resilient to common outdoor challenges. Whether you’re growing indoors or outdoors, Night Moves Strain is relatively straightforward to cultivate, making it accessible to growers at various experience levels.
