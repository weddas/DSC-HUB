Information on Blueberry

Blueberry from Dutch Passion is a mostly Indica strain (80% Indica, 20% Sativa) that dates back to the late 1970's. A high yielder under optimal conditions. This a thick and stout plant with red, purple and blue shades that acquire a lavender tone. The final product has a very fruity and blueberry aroma.

It produces a longlasting and pleasantly euphoric high of the best quality.

Blueberry has a high percentage of THC = 19.5%.
